# Pooya Labs — Brand system

A restrained identity for an independent engineering practice. The visual language should communicate precision, maturity, clarity, and durable craft—not novelty for its own sake.

## Positioning

**Pooya Labs** is the personal engineering brand of Pooya Goodarzi.

Core areas:
- Software engineering
- AI engineering and applied AI
- Backend and distributed systems
- Digital product development

The brand should feel like a serious independent engineering studio: technically capable, considered, and direct. Avoid implying a staffed company, commercial scale, or production readiness where those claims are not established.

## Visual principles

1. **Restraint over spectacle.** Use negative space, hierarchy, and typography before decoration.
2. **Durability over trends.** Avoid neon gradients, glowing circuitry, robot imagery, and generic AI motifs.
3. **Precision over ornament.** Prefer simple geometry, fine rules, measured spacing, and aligned layouts.
4. **Human credibility.** Keep Pooya's real portrait as the personal GitHub avatar; use the monogram as a supporting brand mark.
5. **Product independence.** Individual projects may retain their own visual identities while sharing the same standards of clarity and craft.

## Core palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary background | Deep charcoal | `#191D1B` |
| Secondary background | Graphite | `#242925` |
| Primary text | Warm ivory | `#E9E4D9` |
| Secondary text | Muted stone | `#AAA99F` |
| Accent | Aged brass | `#B49A70` |
| Subtle rule | Brass at low opacity | `#B49A70` |

Use brass sparingly for fine lines, small labels, and a single point of emphasis. It is not a bright highlight color.

## Typography

- Display: a restrained editorial serif or a high-quality neutral sans-serif.
- Body: a highly legible modern sans-serif.
- Use sentence case for descriptions and uppercase sparingly for compact labels.
- Avoid excessive letter spacing in body copy and avoid all-caps paragraphs.

## Voice

Write with a direct, technically grounded, measured tone.

Prefer:
- “I design and build…”
- “The project explores…”
- “Current implementation…”
- “Experimental; not production-ready.”

Avoid:
- Inflated claims such as “world-class”, “revolutionary”, or “industry-leading”.
- Overly enthusiastic startup language.
- Long lists of technologies without context.
- Presenting plans as completed capabilities.

## Bilingual content

- English first, Persian second, unless the audience or page is specifically Persian.
- Keep both versions equivalent in meaning and factual claims.
- Use natural Persian, with right-to-left formatting where the platform supports it.
- Preserve product and technology names in their original spelling.

## Asset usage

- `assets/pooya-labs-header.svg`: profile README header.
- `assets/pooya-labs-mark.svg`: supporting monogram for future brand surfaces.
- The profile avatar remains Pooya's real photograph.

## Do not

- Add neon cyan/purple glow effects.
- Use stock AI robot heads, circuit-brain illustrations, or random abstract gradients.
- Add decorative badges, activity counters, or skill bars solely to fill space.
- Apply the Pooya Labs mark to another project's interface without a deliberate design decision.
